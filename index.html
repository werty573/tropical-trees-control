Questions

Do you come out to see the job first?

Yes. Send a few photos on WhatsApp for a quick idea, and we arrange a visit to look at the trees and give you a price.

Can you take down a tree close to my house?

Yes. Trees near roofs, wires and walls are cut in sections and lowered by rope, so they come down in a controlled way.

How do I know if I have termites?

Look for mud tubes on trunks or walls, hollow-sounding wood, small piles of droppings and winged insects after rain. If you see any of these, message us.

Do you take away the cut wood?

Clean-up and removal of branches and logs can be included in your quote. Just tell us when you message.

Which areas do you cover?

Residential, commercial and industrial properties throughout Trinidad.
